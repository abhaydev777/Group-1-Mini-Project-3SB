Department Attendance Policy

Students must maintain consistent participation across all registered courses. Attendance is calculated from the first day of instruction through the last teaching day before examinations. Medical leave must be documented and approved by the departmental office within seven working days.

Minimum requirement: 75% overall attendance to remain exam-eligible.
