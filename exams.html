Examination Guidelines & Regulations

All candidates must comply with the following rules. Violations may result in cancellation of the paper and disciplinary action.

  • Valid ID mandatory: Carry your university identity card and printed / digital admit card. Entry will be denied without both documents.
  • Entry closes 15 minutes prior: Gates shut at the published start time minus 15 minutes. Late arrivals will not be admitted under any circumstances.
  • No electronic devices: Mobiles, smartwatches, calculators (unless explicitly permitted), and earphones are prohibited inside the hall.
